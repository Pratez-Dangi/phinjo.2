Stage 05

FIND THE STRONGHOLD

Throw an Eye of Ender and follow its direction. Keep track of your route and gather enough resources for the return journey.

01

Follow the Eyes

Eyes of Ender float toward a nearby Stronghold. They may break when thrown, so bring spares.

  • Throw an Eye and note the direction it travels.
  • Move forward and throw another after travelling a reasonable distance.
  • When the Eye starts moving downwards, search underground nearby.
  • Explore the Stronghold carefully until you locate the portal room.
02

Activate the End portal

Place Eyes of Ender into any empty portal-frame sockets until the portal activates.

  • Light the room and block off nearby mob routes if needed.
  • Check every portal frame and fill any empty socket.
  • Store spare equipment safely if you have a secure room.
  • Take one last look at food, armor, bow, arrows, and blocks before jumping in.
Entering the End is a major step. The exit portal only becomes available after the dragon is defeated, so bring gear you can afford to lose.

Portal fact

An End portal frame has 12 sockets. A naturally generated portal can have some sockets already filled, so the number of Eyes you need may be lower than 12.

Stage 06

DEFEAT THE DRAGON

Keep moving, watch the dragon's flight path, and focus on the End Crystals before trying to deal consistent damage.

03

First: deal with the End Crystals

The crystals on top of obsidian pillars heal the dragon. Destroying them makes the fight more manageable.

  • Use a bow to destroy crystals you can safely hit from the ground.
  • Be careful when approaching iron-bar cages around some crystals.
  • Do not stand directly beside a crystal when it is destroyed.
  • Use blocks carefully if you need to climb a pillar, and plan your descent.
04

Damage the dragon safely

Keep watching your health, avoid standing still for too long, and attack when the dragon is vulnerable near the central exit fountain.

  • Stay alert to knockback and the dragon's breath attack.
  • Keep food ready and retreat briefly if health gets low.
  • Use your bow at range and your sword when a safe opening appears.
  • After victory, collect experience and use the exit portal to return.
Remember: Endermen are common in the End. Avoid looking directly at their faces unless you are prepared to fight.
