Stage 03

OPEN THE NETHER GATE

The Nether is dangerous terrain. Go in prepared and know how to get back before exploring far from your entry point.

01

Build and light the portal

A standard vertical Nether portal has an outer frame at least 4 blocks wide and 5 blocks tall.

  • Build the frame with obsidian. The four corner blocks can be omitted.
  • Ignite the interior with flint and steel or another valid fire source.
  • Stand clear and enter only after checking your inventory.
  • Record the Overworld portal coordinates before moving far away.
02

Build a safe landing zone

Your first few blocks can make the difference between a controlled visit and a lost inventory.

  • Block off exposed edges and nearby hazards.
  • Use obvious markers to guide you back to the portal.
  • Carry spare blocks for bridges and emergency cover.
  • Watch for lava, ghasts, piglins, and unexpected drops.
Watch out: Water does not behave normally in the Nether and cannot be placed as a regular source block there. A fire resistance potion can make lava emergencies more survivable.

Useful rule

Stage 04

BLAZE RODS & ENDER PEARLS

Eyes of Ender are made using blaze powder and Ender Pearls. You need these to locate the Stronghold and activate its End portal.

03

Find a Nether fortress

Fortresses can be difficult to spot. Travel carefully, keep a route back, and do not rush into large groups of hostile mobs.

  • Explore from a protected portal area.
  • Use bridges and markers to avoid getting lost.
  • Look for blaze spawners and prepare before engaging.
  • Collect blaze rods, then craft them into blaze powder.
04

Collect Ender Pearls

Endermen can drop pearls. Piglin bartering is another possible route, depending on your world and resources.

  • Collect pearls safely when you can.
  • Combine one blaze powder with one Ender Pearl to craft an Eye of Ender.
  • Carry extra Eyes: some can break when thrown, and you may need several to locate the Stronghold.
Plan for more than 12 Eyes of Ender if possible. A fully empty End portal frame can require 12 Eyes, but some frames generate with Eyes already installed.
