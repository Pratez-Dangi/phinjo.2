Field notes

Mining is safer with a plan.

Bring light, food, spare pickaxes, and blocks for bridging or covering holes. Diamond ore becomes more common at lower elevations, but lava and cave hazards are also important risks.

Quick rule: Write down your base coordinates and avoid digging straight down.
