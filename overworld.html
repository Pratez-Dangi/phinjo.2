Stage 01

SURVIVE THE FIRST DAY

Your first goal is not a perfect base. It is a safe night, a reliable food source, and a working set of tools.

01

Collect wood and craft tools

Turn logs into planks and sticks. A crafting table unlocks most early recipes.

  • Collect several logs from nearby trees.
  • Craft planks, a crafting table, and wooden tools.
  • Use a wooden pickaxe to collect cobblestone, then upgrade to stone tools.
  • Make a furnace and cook food when possible.
02

Find food and shelter

A small, well-lit shelter is enough for the first night.

  • Collect food from safe nearby sources.
  • Build a compact shelter or block off a cave entrance.
  • Light the area to reduce hostile mob spawns nearby.
  • Gather three wool and make a bed when you can.
Watch out: Do not assume a cave is safe because its entrance is bright. Carry a shield and keep an escape route in mind.

Stage 02

MINE IRON & DIAMONDS

Upgrade your gear before searching for diamonds. Ore distribution changes with elevation, and a little preparation prevents long, risky trips.

03

Smelt iron and upgrade

Iron is useful for armor, shields, buckets, and a pickaxe that can mine diamond ore.

  • Mine iron ore and smelt it in a furnace.
  • Craft an iron pickaxe before collecting diamonds.
  • Make a shield and upgrade your armor when resources allow.
  • Keep a spare pickaxe and enough food for the return trip.
04

Search lower caves carefully

For modern Overworld generation, diamonds are more common at lower Y-levels, with Y -59 a common mining reference.

Before building a Nether portal: Obsidian requires a diamond pickaxe to drop as an item. A minimum portal uses 10 obsidian blocks if the corners are omitted.
